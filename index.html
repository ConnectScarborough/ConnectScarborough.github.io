Scarborough's students, connected.

A network for high schoolers across Scarborough to meet people outside their own school, share what they're working on, and find out what's actually going on around them.

Join Connect Scarborough See what we do

Two students,
one shared problem.

We're two high schoolers in Scarborough who kept noticing the same thing: there are talented, curious students all over this part of the city, but almost no way to meet the ones outside your own school.

Connect Scarborough is our attempt to fix that — a network built by students, for students, so a good idea, a skill, or an opportunity in one school doesn't stay stuck there.

What we do

Three ways we bring people together.

Meetups

Casual, low-pressure meetups across Scarborough where students from different schools actually talk to each other.

Skill shares

Student-led workshops — coding, art, public speaking, whatever someone in the network is good at and wants to teach.

Opportunities

A shared feed of clubs, competitions, volunteer spots, and events worth knowing about — before they fill up.
